SingLift Sustain

A quiet Android tool for measuring vocal sustain during long-tone practice.

Built for observation, not noise.

SingLift Sustain measures stable long-tone duration and keeps the experience focused on measurement results, local history, and personal best.

SingLift Sustain includes 5 measurement days. Continued use can be unlocked with a one-time Full Unlock. There is no subscription, no ads, no audio recording, and no cloud audio upload.
